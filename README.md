## Approach

Each project follows a structured approach:

1. **Identify** — Understand the business problem and the people affected.
2. **Analyze** — Examine existing processes, requirements and constraints.
3. **Design** — Define the solution, user journey and product requirements.
4. **Build** — Develop and test a functional prototype where appropriate.
5. **Evaluate** — Document outcomes, limitations and opportunities for improvement.

The objective is not technology for its own sake, but practical solutions grounded in real business needs.
